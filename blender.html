My project is a 3D model of a Japanese-style katana sword created in Blender. It features a sharp blade with a unique metal stitched design along one side, giving it a detailed and realistic look. My goal was to improve my 3D modelling skills while creating a high-quality model. To make the katana, I used Blender tools such as Extrude, Loop Cut, Bevel, Mirror Modifier, and Subdivision Surface to shape, smooth, and add detail to the model. I also used Scale, Rotate, and Move to position each part accurately. I put a lot of time and effort into this project, carefully designing every part of the sword to make it look realistic and well-made. This project helped me develop my Blender modelling skills and learn new techniques.
